Read:
- CHATGPT_RADAR_PROMPT.txt for opportunity-discovery definitions and constraints.

- every assigned docs/batches/batch-XXX.json from that same index.

Capture these immutable input identifiers from the index:

IMPORTANT OVERRIDE:
You are NOT the final radar. Do NOT process batches outside your assignment.
Do NOT perform final competitor research. Do NOT update idea_history.json or
idea_catalog.json. Your job is exhaustive discovery over your assigned feed records.

For EVERY feed record in the assigned batches:
1. Count it as triaged exactly once.
2. Use title, summary, source and date for a LIGHT relevance/development pass.
3. Reject obvious noise (sports, celebrity, generic politics, entertainment,
   product-review noise, duplicate category/index pages) without opening the article.
4. If it may contain a capability/regulation/data/infrastructure/cost/behavior/
   workflow delta that could create a commercial web-app opportunity, resolve and
   read the original/canonical article or a reliable primary/corroborating source.
5. From verified candidate articles perform:
   EVENT → BEFORE → AFTER → DELTA → affected workflow → concrete friction/JTBD
   → target customer/buyer → product mechanism → raw opportunity.
6. Generate ALL distinct raw opportunities supported by the assigned material.
   Do not rank and do not stop at a target count.
7. A candidate article may produce multiple distinct raw opportunities.
8. Cross-article combinations are allowed only among articles in your assigned batches;
   the merge stage will later perform dataset-wide combinations.

Write exactly one file:
