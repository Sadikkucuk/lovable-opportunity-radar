STAGE 2 — NOVELTY EXTRACTION
--------------------------------------------------

Determine the actual DELTA created by the article.

Express it explicitly as:

AFTER:
What has become possible, cheaper, faster, mandatory, available,
automatable, measurable, scalable or commercially viable?

DELTA:
AFTER minus BEFORE.

The DELTA is the primary innovation signal.

==================================================
HARD BUILD CONSTRAINTS
==================================================

Every fully qualified idea must satisfy ALL of these constraints:

1. The first commercially usable MVP must be realistically buildable with a TOTAL budget of no more than 500 Lovable credits.
2. Outside Lovable, cash spending must be exactly $0 / €0 / ₺0 for the MVP.
3. Do not require any paid API, paid database, paid hosting, paid domain, paid email infrastructure, paid LLM/API inference, paid data feed, paid scraping service, paid automation platform, paid authentication provider, paid analytics service, paid storage, or any other paid dependency.
4. Free tiers are allowed only when they are sufficient to build and operate the MVP at initial launch without payment.
5. Open-source software, public datasets, free APIs, browser-native capabilities, GitHub/GitHub Actions, free hosting tiers, free database tiers, free authentication tiers and other genuinely no-cost resources may be used.
6. Prefer architectures that can remain at zero external cash cost until the product gets its first paying customers.
7. If a core function cannot work without a paid dependency, do not treat the idea as eligible.
8. No custom domain is required for the MVP; a free Lovable-provided or other free subdomain is acceptable.
9. Avoid products that require custom hardware, physical inventory, regulated licenses, a large operations team, or substantial manual service delivery before the software itself creates value.
10. The MVP must be achievable primarily by one founder using Lovable, with only lightweight coding/configuration outside Lovable when necessary.

==================================================
COMPETITION HARD CONSTRAINTS
==================================================

Every fully qualified idea must also satisfy BOTH conditions below:

A. TÜRKİYE
There must be ZERO identifiable DIRECT competitors in Türkiye.

B. GLOBAL
There must be no more than THREE identifiable DIRECT competitors worldwide.

For competition analysis, "direct competitor" means a product or service whose primary value proposition solves substantially the same core problem for substantially the same target customer with a substantially similar workflow.

Do NOT automatically count the following as direct competitors unless they offer a dedicated product that performs the same core job:
- generic ERP/CRM systems,
- spreadsheets,
- generic project-management tools,
- broad AI assistants,
- general-purpose automation platforms,
- consulting firms,
- large horizontal SaaS platforms with only loosely related features.

COMPETITOR VERIFICATION IS MANDATORY
For EACH candidate idea, perform fresh web research before placing it in the fully qualified list.

Search at minimum:
- Turkish-language queries,
- English-language queries,
- terminology used by the target industry,
- product directories/startup databases that are publicly searchable,
- relevant app/SaaS marketplaces when applicable.

Do not claim "no competitor" merely because the first search returns nothing.
Try multiple descriptions, synonyms, customer-job phrases and category names.

Only classify a competitor as direct when it matches the direct-competitor test above.

If competition cannot be verified with reasonable confidence, mark the idea:
COMPETITION STATUS: NOT VERIFIED
and keep it separate from the fully qualified opportunity list.

Do not silently assume that an idea has zero competitors.
